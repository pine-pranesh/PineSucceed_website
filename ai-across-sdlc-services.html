AI Readiness and Risk Assessment

We evaluate the organization's readiness to use AI in engineering workflows, including data access, source-code sensitivity, infrastructure, governance, and team adoption.

  • Privacy and intellectual property risks

  • Team skills and change readiness
  • Baseline delivery metrics

Common priority use cases include:

  • Requirements and user story assistance
  • Developer coding assistants
  • Automated code review
  • Test generation and maintenance
  • Defect triage
  • Build and deployment analysis
  • Incident and knowledge assistance

  • Integration approach
  • Access and data-control model
  • Human review workflow
  • Quality and safety guardrails
  • KPIs and adoption plan
