Testing and Quality Assurance

  • Role and permission testing

  • Performance and security testing
  • Accessibility and SEO checks
  • User acceptance testing

Deployment and Launch

Step 07 / 08

Deployment and Launch

PineSucceed prepares the production environment, deploys the website, validates the launch, and supports a controlled transition from the existing platform.

Launch activities include:

  • Redirect and analytics verification

  • Go-live monitoring
