Our IT Consulting
Process

Step 01

Discovery and Business Analysis

Step 01 / 08

Discovery and Business Analysis

We begin by understanding your business model, objectives, operating processes, technology landscape, and constraints.

Key activities:

  • Leadership and stakeholder discussions
  • Business and operational requirement analysis
  • Application and infrastructure discovery
  • Data and integration review
  • Security and compliance discovery
  • Cost and vendor review
  • Success criteria definition
Step 02

Current-State Assessment

Step 02 / 08

Current-State Assessment

We evaluate the existing environment across architecture, applications, infrastructure, data, security, and operations.

Assessment areas include:

  • Application portfolio and criticality
  • Infrastructure and hosting condition
  • Integration and data flow landscape
  • Security and compliance posture
  • Operational and support maturity
  • Licensing, vendor, and cost structure
  • Technical debt and key risks
Step 03

Gap Analysis and Recommendations

Step 03 / 08

Gap Analysis and Recommendations

We compare the current state against business requirements and good practice to identify gaps and practical options.

Key deliverables include:

  • Gap analysis against requirements
  • Prioritized findings by impact and risk
  • Solution and architecture options
  • Effort, cost, and risk estimates
  • Build, buy, or partner recommendations
  • Quick wins and long-term actions
  • Decision-support documentation
Step 04

Target Architecture and Roadmap

Step 04 / 08

Target Architecture and Roadmap

We define the target technology state and a sequenced plan to reach it without unnecessary disruption.

Roadmap components include:

  • Target architecture and standards
  • Application and platform direction
  • Modernization and migration sequencing
  • Data and integration plan
  • Security and governance approach
  • Investment and resource planning
  • Delivery phases and milestones
Step 05

Solution and Initiative Design

Step 05 / 08

Solution and Initiative Design

For prioritized initiatives, we produce detailed designs that are ready for implementation or vendor engagement.

Design deliverables include:

  • Solution and application design
  • Integration and data design
  • Security and access design
  • Infrastructure and cloud design
  • Implementation and testing approach
  • Operating and support model
  • Requirement and scope documentation
Step 06

Implementation Guidance and Oversight

Step 06 / 08

Implementation Guidance and Oversight

We support delivery through technical guidance, review, governance, and quality assurance rather than leaving recommendations unimplemented.

Support activities include:

  • Technical guidance for internal teams
  • Vendor and delivery oversight
  • Architecture and design review
  • Program governance and reporting
  • Quality and testing review
  • Risk and issue management
  • Change and dependency coordination
Step 07

Validation and Review

Step 07 / 08

Validation and Review

We review delivered solutions against the intended architecture, requirements, security, and performance expectations.

Review activities include:

  • Design and architecture conformance review
  • Functional and integration validation
  • Security and access verification
  • Performance and capacity review
  • Documentation and operation readiness
  • Go-live readiness assessment
  • Post-implementation review

Continuous Advisory and Optimization

After delivery, we help maintain momentum through ongoing advisory, optimization, and roadmap refinement.

Ongoing activities include:
