Our Approach

Our CRM Consulting
Process

Step 01

Discovery and Business Analysis

Step 01 / 08

Discovery and Business Analysis

We begin by understanding your customers, revenue processes, teams, systems, data, challenges, and expectations from the CRM.

Key activities:

  • Stakeholder discussions
  • Business and customer process analysis
  • Sales, marketing, and service review
  • Existing system and data assessment
  • Integration and reporting inventory
  • Pain point identification
  • Success criteria definition
Step 02

Requirement Definition and Process Design

Step 02 / 08

Requirement Definition and Process Design

We document the target processes, data requirements, user roles, integrations, and reporting needs that the CRM must support.

Key deliverables include:

  • Functional requirement documentation
  • Target sales and service processes
  • Data and record model definition
  • User role and permission matrix
  • Integration and reporting requirements
  • Migration scope definition
  • Prioritized feature list
Step 03

CRM Platform Selection

Step 03 / 08

CRM Platform Selection

We evaluate candidate platforms against requirements, process fit, customization needs, integration capability, cost, and long-term ownership.

Selection activities include:

  • Platform and licensing comparison
  • Fit and gap analysis
  • Customization and development assessment
  • Integration capability review
  • Total cost of ownership estimation
  • Risk and dependency review
  • Recommendation and decision support
Step 04

Solution Design and Configuration

Step 04 / 08

Solution Design and Configuration

We design and configure the CRM structure, automation, interfaces, security, and integrations according to the approved requirements.

Design and configuration activities include:

  • Environment and application setup
  • Objects, modules, and field design
  • Pipeline and process configuration
  • Automation and workflow implementation
  • Roles, permissions, and sharing rules
  • Integration and API configuration
  • Layout and user experience design
Step 05

Data Migration and Integration

Step 05 / 08

Data Migration and Integration

We move customer data from legacy systems into the CRM and connect the platform with the systems that support your operations.

Key activities include:

  • Source data profiling and cleaning
  • Deduplication and standardization
  • Field mapping and transformation
  • Account and contact structure migration
  • Historical data migration
  • Integration implementation and testing
  • Data validation and reconciliation
Step 06

Testing and User Acceptance

Step 06 / 08

Testing and User Acceptance

We test the CRM for functional correctness, process alignment, integrations, permissions, data accuracy, and usability.

Validation includes:

  • Functional and process testing
  • Automation and workflow testing
  • Integration and data synchronization testing
  • Permission and visibility testing
  • Report and dashboard validation
  • Performance and usability checks
  • User acceptance testing
Step 07

Training and Go-Live

Step 07 / 08

Training and Go-Live

We prepare users, documentation, and support processes, then launch the CRM in a controlled manner.

Go-live activities include:

  • Production environment preparation
  • Final data migration and validation
  • Role-based user training
  • Documentation and user guides
  • Legacy system transition planning
  • Go-live monitoring and support
  • Post-launch issue resolution
Step 08

Adoption, Optimization, and Support

Step 08 / 08

Adoption, Optimization, and Support

After launch, we monitor usage, refine configuration, improve data quality, and extend the CRM as business needs change.

Ongoing activities include:

  • Adoption tracking and reporting
  • User feedback and usability refinement
  • Data quality maintenance
  • Process and automation improvements
  • Reporting and dashboard enhancement
  • Platform update management
  • Feature roadmap development
