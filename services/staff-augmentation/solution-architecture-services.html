Discovery and Architecture Context

Architecture Assessment

  • Security and access posture
  • Resilience and recovery readiness
  • Technical debt and architectural risk

Step 03 / 08

Requirements and Quality Attribute Analysis

  • Assumption recording
  • Architecture driver identification

Step 06 / 08

Architecture Patterns, Standards,
and Technologies

Application Patterns

Layered, modular, hexagonal, event-driven, and service-oriented architectures

Distributed Patterns

Microservices, service mesh, API gateway, and saga or orchestration patterns

Integration Patterns

Synchronous APIs, messaging, event streaming, and batch integration

Data Patterns

Relational and NoSQL stores, data lakes, warehouses, and CQRS approaches

Cloud Platforms

AWS, Microsoft Azure, and Google Cloud services and architectures

Containers

Docker, Kubernetes, and container hosting and orchestration platforms

Security Patterns

Zero trust principles, identity federation, encryption, and secrets management

Resilience Patterns

Redundancy, circuit breakers, bulkheads, retries, and graceful degradation

Observability

Metrics, logging, tracing, alerting, and health monitoring

Modelling Notations

C4-style diagrams, UML, BPMN where applicable, and decision records

Our Approach

Why Choose PineSucceed for Solution Architecture?

Our architects understand commercial objectives, operational constraints, and delivery realities as well as technical patterns, so designs are practical.

We translate requirements, including non-functional quality attributes, into explicit architectural decisions rather than assuming them.

PineSucceed combines architecture with application development, integration, data, cloud, security, and DevOps experience, so designs account for implementation.

We produce diagrams, specifications, and decision records that development teams actually use, rather than high-level diagrams nobody references.

Where architectural assumptions carry significant risk, we validate them with proof of concept or technical spike work before the build depends on them.

Because most failures happen at interfaces and in data, we treat integration, API, and data architecture as first-class design concerns.

We help establish architecture principles, review processes, and conformance practice so systems stay coherent as many teams deliver change.

Beyond initial design, PineSucceed supports delivery guidance, conformance review, technology assessment, and ongoing architecture evolution.
