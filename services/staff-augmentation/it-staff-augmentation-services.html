Our Staff Augmentation
Process

Step 01

Requirement Discovery

Step 01 / 08

Requirement Discovery

We begin by understanding your project, team, technology, timeline, and the constraint you need to remove.

Key activities:

  • Stakeholder discussions
  • Project and roadmap review
  • Technology and platform assessment
  • Existing team capability analysis
  • Delivery process and tooling review
  • Constraint and gap identification
  • Success criteria definition
Step 02

Role and Profile Definition

Step 02 / 08

Role and Profile Definition

We define the role precisely, including skills, seniority, responsibilities, and engagement terms.

Definition deliverables include:

  • Role description and responsibilities
  • Required technical skills and experience
  • Seniority and autonomy expectations
  • Working model and reporting line
  • Engagement duration and availability
  • Security and compliance requirements
  • Rate and commercial terms
Step 03

Sourcing and Screening

Step 03 / 08

Sourcing and Screening

We identify professionals whose background genuinely matches the requirement and screen them before submission.

Sourcing activities include:

  • Skill and experience matching
  • Domain and technology relevance check
  • Technical capability screening
  • Communication and collaboration assessment
  • Availability confirmation
  • Reference and background verification
  • Profile preparation and submission
Step 04

Client Review and Selection

Step 04 / 08

Client Review and Selection

You review proposed profiles and assess suitability before confirming the engagement.

Selection activities include:

  • Profile review by your team
  • Technical or domain discussions
  • Role fit and collaboration assessment
  • Adjustment of requirement if needed
  • Candidate confirmation
  • Start date agreement
  • Replacement terms clarification
Step 05

Onboarding and Access Setup

Step 05 / 08

Onboarding and Access Setup

We integrate the professional into your environment with the access, context, and orientation they need.

Onboarding activities include:

  • Project and domain orientation
  • Codebase and system walkthrough
  • Repository, tool, and environment access
  • Process and meeting cadence alignment
  • Security and confidentiality briefing
  • Task and milestone planning
  • Communication channel setup
Step 06

Delivery Contribution

Step 06 / 08

Delivery Contribution

The professional works within your team on your priorities and deliverables.

Delivery activities include:

  • Sprint or task execution
  • Development, testing, or specialist work
  • Participation in reviews and planning
  • Standards and documentation compliance
  • Collaboration with internal staff
  • Progress updates
  • Issue escalation when required
Step 07

Engagement Review and Support

Step 07 / 08

Engagement Review and Support

We check that the engagement is working and adjust where necessary.

Review activities include:

  • Contribution and effectiveness review
  • Stakeholder feedback collection
  • Working practice adjustment
  • Role or skill refinement
  • Issue resolution support
  • Capacity adjustment planning
  • Extension or closure discussion
Step 08

Transition and Closure

Step 08 / 08

Transition and Closure

When the requirement ends or changes, we manage the transition carefully to protect continuity.

Closure activities include:

  • Structured knowledge transfer
  • Documentation completion
  • Handover to internal staff or replacement
  • Access and credential revocation
  • Asset and tooling return
  • Final engagement review
  • Long-term partnership planning
