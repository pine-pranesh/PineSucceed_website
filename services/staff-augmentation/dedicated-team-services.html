Engagement Process

Step 01

Discovery and Requirement Analysis

Step 01 / 08

Discovery and Requirement Analysis

We begin by understanding your objectives, project, technology stack, timeline, and internal capabilities.

Key activities:

  • Stakeholder discussions
  • Project and product scope review
  • Technology and architecture assessment
  • Delivery process and tooling review
  • Existing team and capability analysis
  • Constraint and dependency identification
  • Success criteria definition

Team Composition Planning

Step 02 / 08

Team Composition Planning

We define the roles, seniority, and structure the engagement requires.

Planning deliverables include:

  • Role and skill matrix
  • Team size and structure
  • Reporting and leadership model
  • Engagement model definition
  • Governance and communication plan
  • Ramp-up approach and timeline
  • Commercial and contractual terms

Candidate Matching and Selection

Step 03 / 08

Candidate Matching and Selection

We propose engineers whose experience fits your project, then give you the opportunity to assess them.

Matching activities include:

  • Skill and experience matching
  • Relevant domain or technology background
  • Profile submission and review
  • Technical discussions and interviews
  • Selection confirmation
  • Availability and start date agreement
  • Replacement commitment if required

Onboarding and Environment Setup

Step 04 / 08

Onboarding and Environment Setup

We integrate the team into your environment, tooling, and processes with a structured onboarding plan.

Onboarding activities include:

  • Domain and project orientation
  • Codebase and architecture walkthrough
  • Repository, tool, and access provisioning
  • Process and workflow alignment
  • Security and confidentiality briefing
  • Communication and meeting cadence setup
  • Initial milestone and task planning

Delivery Execution

  • Code review and documentation
  • Increment demonstration
  • Progress and risk reporting

Step 06 / 08

Quality and Governance Oversight

  • Performance and reliability review
  • Technical debt tracking
  • Escalation and resolution support

Step 07 / 08

Performance Review and Improvement

We review delivery effectiveness regularly and adjust team composition or practice where needed.

Review activities include:

  • Delivery performance review
  • Quality and defect trend analysis
  • Team productivity assessment
  • Process improvement actions
  • Role and capacity adjustment
  • Stakeholder feedback integration
  • Engagement health checks

Step 08 / 08

Scaling, Transition, and Continuity

As your needs change, we scale the team, adjust its shape, or plan a structured transition.

  • Handover planning and execution
  • Long-term roadmap alignment
  • Engagement renewal planning
