  • Application and architecture review

  • Tooling and environment inventory

  • Operational and incident review
  • Success criteria definition

DevOps Maturity Evaluation

Step 02 / 08

DevOps Maturity Evaluation

We assess delivery and operations maturity against practical industry benchmarks and identify priority improvement areas.

Assessment areas include:

  • Deployment frequency and lead time
  • Change failure and recovery rates
  • Build and test automation coverage
  • Environment consistency and provisioning
  • Configuration and secrets management
  • Security and compliance integration
  • Monitoring, alerting, and incident practice

Step 03 / 08

Roadmap components include:

  • Environment and infrastructure strategy
  • Security and quality integration plan
  • Team structure and skills plan
  • Delivery phases and milestones

Why Choose PineSucceed for DevOps Services?

PineSucceed supports assessment, strategy, pipeline build, infrastructure automation, containerization, security, monitoring, rollout, and ongoing support.

Our team combines DevOps practice with application development, cloud engineering, databases, APIs, testing, and security expertise.

We automate what creates real benefit for your teams and applications, and leave process complexity out of the pipeline.

Secrets management, scanning, policy enforcement, and access control are built into pipelines and infrastructure rather than added afterwards.
